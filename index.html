Can I order the extender now?

No. This is a demo store and we're not accepting orders or payments. The $89.90 USD price is an estimate, not a confirmed offer.

Will it fit my car and my dog?

Fit, dimensions and weight capacity are not yet verified. Before launch, we'll publish confirmed measurements and compatibility guidance. Do not assume a universal fit.

Does it protect my dog in an accident?

No crash protection or safety certification is claimed. A seat extender is not a crash restraint. Use an independently verified pet restraint appropriate for your dog and vehicle.

Is it waterproof and easy to clean?

Materials, water resistance and cleaning instructions need to be verified on a real sample. We'll share confirmed care information before orders open.

Where will you ship?

Shipping regions, costs and timelines are still to be confirmed. Worldwide delivery is not promised.

Are these photos of the final product?

No. All visuals are original concept illustrations used as placeholders. Final photography will be added after sample approval and image rights are confirmed.
